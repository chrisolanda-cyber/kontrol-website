Chris Olanda is the founder of Kontrol AI, a Gold Coast AI workflow automation consultancy operating as CO Advisory Pty Ltd (ABN 85 693 421 788). He builds automation for Australian trade contractors and professional services firms, connecting existing platforms such as email, SimPRO, Xero, ServiceM8 and CRMs so that job intake, quoting, dispatch and invoicing run without manual re-keying.

Before founding Kontrol AI in 2026, Chris worked in risk, operations and digital transformation at Macquarie Bank, redesigning the controls and processes behind financial operations at scale. He holds a Master of Business. That background is why Kontrol AI builds for efficiency, impact and safety: every automation carries a full audit trail, is robust, and anything ambiguous is routed to a human instead of guessed at.

Live numbers from those production pipelines are published on the home page and refreshed daily.

Kontrol AI is based on the Gold Coast, serves Brisbane and South East Queensland, and works with clients nationally.
