Built for the Australian stack

Automation designed around a US software stack tends to assume different tax handling, different payroll cycles and different privacy rules. The work here is built around SimPRO, ServiceM8, Xero and MYOB, with GST-correct invoicing and Australian payroll timing.

Australian businesses handling personal information sit under the Privacy Act 1988 and the Australian Privacy Principles. From 10 December 2026, businesses using automated decision making that significantly affects individuals must disclose it in their privacy policy — an obligation that arrives whether or not a consultant mentions it.

How to judge any automation consultant

Three questions worth asking whoever you talk to, here or elsewhere. Will they show you a live system they have actually built? Will they name the tools they use and the platform it runs on? Will they put a real number behind a result, with the period and the volume behind it?

A consultant who can't answer all three is asking you to take the interesting part on faith.
